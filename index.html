A personal upload script for one YouTube channel

Land and Reason Uploader is a small command-line script that the owner of the Land and Reason YouTube channel runs on their own computer to upload their own videos to that channel.

It is not a public app or service. There is nothing to download or sign up for, and nobody other than the channel owner can sign in to it. This page exists because Google asks every app that uses Google sign-in to have a home page and a privacy policy.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file and its thumbnail image to the owner's channel.
  • Sets that video's title, description, tags, category, visibility and scheduled publish time.
  • Checks whether an uploaded video has finished processing.
  • Reads the channel's own video list and public counters (views, subscribers) for a status report.
  • Can replace the channel's banner image when the owner asks it to.

Why it asks for Google access

YouTube only accepts uploads from a signed-in channel owner. When the owner runs the script for the first time, Google shows its consent screen and the owner allows two permissions for their own channel:

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage the channel's own videos and settings over a secure connection.

The script talks to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API and to nothing else.

What happens to data

The sign-in key that Google issues is kept in a file on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone. The details are in the privacy policy.
